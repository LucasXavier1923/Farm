# A Day on the Farm (Meta 11)

The daily loop is continuous and co-op safe: opening a menu never pauses farm
time. The host clock advances the world; peers render host snapshots.

## Player rhythm

- Morning: weather, animals, crops, and orders establish the day's priorities.
- Daytime: farming, harvesting, gathering, production, and building use energy.
- Evening: the HUD advises wrapping up, but does not force sleep or block play.
- Night: lanterns preserve visibility and the player may continue working.
- Sleep: an explicit ready vote advances the day only when every session member
  is ready. In solo it remains a one-player confirmation.

`FarmDailyRhythm` is presentation guidance only. It is deliberately separate
from time, inventory, and co-op authority so artists can change its wording and
future accessibility settings without changing gameplay simulation.
