# Farmhouse Comfort, Rest, and Meaningful Recovery

Meta 39 adds a shared Evening Tea ritual at the bed.

- A farm with one `Wildflower Tea` may prepare Comfort before sleeping.
- The preparation is host-routed through `RestRecovery`, consumes the tea once, and persists in save version 36.
- At the next morning, the farm receives three Comfort charges.
- Each charge reduces one Energy cost by one, then is consumed. This complements food and does not replace the Energy refill from sleep.
- The bed modal and Energy HUD explain the current evening plan and remaining Comfort charges. World time still does not pause while UI is open.

Validation: `META39_40_EVENING_SMOKE` verified save/restore, morning activation, a Hoe cost reduced from 4 to 3, and the remaining two charges.
