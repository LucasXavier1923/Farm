# Live Systems Map

The living FigJam map for the playable 3D co-op farm prototype is:

<https://www.figma.com/board/r22sF11qfXDnHKkmfOQgvM>

It is the presentation and collaboration companion to the Markdown documentation. It maps the current farm-day loop, activities, economy, progression, host-authoritative co-op boundary, item registry, delivery status, and the remaining live-validation gates.

The lower `Detailed guide` section explains each major system through four questions: what it is, why it exists, what the player does, and what result it feeds into the rest of the farm.

## Updating rule

Update this FigJam whenever a meta adds a player activity, reward, choice, or a material connection to an existing system. Keep future integrations clearly marked as future until their live validation is complete.
